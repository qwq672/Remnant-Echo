### Meet the Piglins
- **来源类型**：minecraft.net
- **原文链接**：<https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/meet-piglins>
- **发布时间**：2020-06-17T14:50:40.687+0000
- **页面摘要**：The gold-hoarding hogs of the Nether

**原文引用：**

> Otherworldly biomes, rich alien wildlife, and never-before-seen blocks just waiting to be mined. The newly updated Nether is a realm of untold resources, but also a realm of troublesome mobs that will make your stay as frustrating as possible. We’re not talking about the Ghasts, Wither skeletons, or Blazes (you wish)! We’re talking about the new faction in town – your nefarious Nether neighbors: the Piglins.

> Who are these obnoxious oinkers, what are they doing in the Nether, and who thought it was a good idea to add competition for our precious resources?!

> “The reason why we added Piglins was because we wanted to bring more life to the Nether, and also to add a bit of culture and humor,” explains Minecraft developer and esteemed Piglinotologist Henrik Kniberg. “As a new player going into the Nether, the Piglins probably kick your butt if you’re not careful!”

> Piglins can be found all over the Nether, especially around bastion remnants where they gather in large numbers. While Piglins may look harmless, they’re extremely protective of what’s theirs and won’t hesitate to give explorers a good bonk on the noggin if they cross a certain line – something that many players have already learned the hard way in recent Snapshots.

> “If you open a chest near a Piglin, they will assume you’re a thief and attack you, even if it’s technically your chest,” says Henrik. “That means you can never completely relax when you’re around them. It’s very easy to forget this, even for me as a developer who coded that feature! But that’s what we want – players not being 100% comfortable around Piglins.”

> Dealing with grumpy neighbors (the kind that arm themselves with golden swords and crossbows) is never easy, but don’t let their unpredictability and overprotective nature discourage you. Piglins may not be as well-mannered as the Overworld’s Villagers, nor will they appreciate you for your witty comments, but there’s one thing they’ll do just about anything for: gold.

> “As a player you want to wear gold to make sure they don’t attack you. You can also use gold ingots for bartering,” says Henrik. “But you should also keep in mind that they will get upset if they find you mining gold, because they think all the gold is theirs. So be careful when mining gold in the Nether.”

> A Piglin’s fascination with gold is just one of the many unique behaviors that they possess. They also hunt Hoglins, battle Wither skeletons, and have a peculiar fear of soul fire. When these behaviors are exposed to the other residents of the Nether, you get all sorts of weird and random encounters.



### Bastion remnant
- **来源类型**：minecraft.net
- **原文链接**：<https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/bastion-remnant>
- **发布时间**：2025-11-15T15:00:00.000+0000
- **页面摘要**：Get your best armor and weaponry together, because you’re about to step inside a Baston remnant!

**原文引用：**

> The Nether is an unpleasant place at the best of times. Soul sand slowing you down. Lava lakes burning you up. Stray ghast fireballs knocking you off things. But the regular ol’ Nether is, quite honestly, a picnic compared to the structure we’re visiting today. Get your best armor and weaponry together, because you’re about to step inside a baston remnant.

> Despite being buried in netherrack, these tall, imposing structures are easy to recognise in the smoky half-light of the Nether. They’re huge – many storeys tall – and made of blackstone and basalt. They’ll also be crawling with piglins. They can be found in almost all Nether biomes, with the sole exception of basalt deltas.

> Bastion remnants began appearing after the release of the Nether Update in June 2020 – the same patch that added piglins, netherite equipment, chains, and target blocks. They come in four different varieties – each with its own structure and loot opportunities: there’s a bridge variant, a variant made up of hoglin stables, one with housing units for piglins, and a treasure room piled high with gold.

> Speaking of which, let’s talk loot. Bastion remnants are the only place where you’ll find a few fancy items – gilded blackstone, Netherite upgrade smithing templates, the snout banner pattern and armor trim, and the Pigstep music disc. But that’s not all you’ll be able to take home. You’ll also find plenty of iron, Netherite and gold, crying obsidian, ancient debris, netherite scrap, and a surprising and concerning amount of cooked porkchops.

> But don’t expect that you’ll just waltz on in and start opening chests. Those goodies are guarded by piglins, piglin brutes, and sometimes hoglins too – not to mention the other denizens of the Nether that might wander by. Come prepared, don’t take too many risks, and you might juuuust make it back home alive.

> A bastion, in the real world, is a type of fortification – specifically one that sticks out from a defensive wall so that the defenders can fire at people trying to climb up or breach the wall. They’re found all over fortifications dating from the mid 16th to the mid 19th centuries – once gunpowder was in use, but before modern artillery.

> Which raises an interesting question – the name “bastion remnant” suggests that the structures found in the Nether might be all that remains of large defensive walls, built in the Nether by an unknown people. Were they trying to keep out the piglins, and did they fail? Or did the piglins build it to keep something else out?

> Only time and patient archaeology will likely reveal the truth. Until then, feel free to loot these ancient structures of their goodies. Just let us know if you find any interesting hints as to their history, yeah?



### Bastion of baddies
- **来源类型**：minecraft.net
- **原文链接**：<https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/bastion-baddies>
- **发布时间**：2024-01-30T15:47:11.840+0000
- **页面摘要**：As a new player to Minecraft, you quickly realize the endless possibilities in building and terraforming – but few know about the ready-made structures spread through the Overworld. In this article, we explore the mysterious and infamous woodland mansion structure.

**原文引用：**

> As a new player to Minecraft, I was aware of the endless possibilities in building and terraforming, but not so much about ready-made structures created by anonymous crafters. One rumour had reached me, though – the elusive and mysterious woodland mansion, and I was eager to find one for myself. Apparently, these mansions are typically found in roofed forest biomes and the road to discovering one is often paved with challenges. Why am I not surprised? Unfortunately, the woodland mansions are not conveniently placed along well-worn paths; rather they’re hidden in dense woodland, (hence the name) and are likely to require you to traverse challenging terrain, cross rivers, and navigate thick foliage. Then once you get there, the mansion’s halls are guarded by hostile illager mobs. Yikes.

> Finding a woodland mansion can be tricky, depending on the mode you’re playing in. If you’re in Survival mode, they may occasionally appear on treasure maps, if you don’t have one of those, you’ll have to traverse the world for the right biome instead. But fortune favours the brave and all that...

> Okay, so I’m not brave, I prefer to play in Creative mode. Call it cowardice, but I like to create limitlessly without some pesky little illager trying to ruin my life. If, like me, you’d rather focus on creating than fighting, then playing in Creative mode is definitely the better option, especially if you’re going to a woodland mansion that’s crawling with the little brutes.

> Creative mode also makes finding one much easier – you can do this by typing in "/locate structure minecraftmansion" in the chat and you’ll instantly receive the coordinates to teleport to your dream home. To teleport, you need to type /teleport into the chat then the coordinates. Just don’t type in the wrong coordinates, or you might end up on a remote island in the middle of the ocean like I did. Oops! Second time lucky, I ended up at a mansion. Well sort of – I actually landed in a pitch-black hole under the mansion that I had to dig myself out of. But hey, close enough!
