> Trial chambers[note 1] are uncommon underground structures that serve as a mid-game combat challenge.[1] They consist mostly of copper blocks and tuff bricks, and are the only place where trial spawners and vaults naturally generate. Trial chambers are also the only place where breezes spawn, whose breeze rods can be used to craft wind charges. Various materials such as heavy cores and certain armor trims are obtained exclusively by unlocking vaults in trial chambers. As it is the only source of breeze rods and heavy cores, the structure is also the only location where maces can be obtained.

> Trial chambers can also be used to obtain copper bulbs without entering the Nether, and large quantities of copper blocks and their derivatives.

> Most copper blocks found in trial chambers are waxed, preventing them from oxidizing. However, some unwaxed copper blocks can also generate due to a bug.

> Ominous trials can also take place here through the Bad Omen effect. Bad Omen converts to Trial Omen, which causes all trial spawners to become ominous, making them more difficult and providing greater rewards.

> Hallways are segments that connect corridors to chambers. When a copper door generates in a corridor, it (or, in the case of an upper or lower door, the spiral staircase that follows it; see § Slices) may lead to one of the following pieces:

> an encounter (0.41%);‌[Java Edition only]

> If the selected piece cannot be generated, the game attempts to generate a different piece. After a certain number of failed attempts, it defaults to generating a dead end.​[more information needed] If the dead end itself cannot be generated, no piece is generated.[3]

> This process is then repeated at the end of each hallway segment or encounter. Furthermore, hallways never branch, meaning that every copper door found in corridors eventually leads to a single chamber, a dead end, or nothing.

> Encounters are hallway pieces that consist in "short challenges leading to a larger chamber".[6] They feature a single trial spawner and a vault.

> Chambers are large combat rooms that contain multiple trial spawners and vaults, as well as one ominous vault. Chambers can be accessed from hallways, intersections, end rooms, and corridor slices.

> Each chamber design has several potential entrances, of which only one is connected to the rest of the structure. The remaining entrances are sealed with an entrance cap (see § Hallways). Each potential entrance, including the sealed ones, generates a disposal consisting of a hopper placed above a barrel (see § Decor), allowing players to manage their inventory before entering a chamber.[7]

> Decor includes decorated pots, flower pots, candles, and barrels. It can generate in slices, some encounters, and undecorated_pot can generate in chamber_4.

> Disposals and beds are also classified as decor but are not part of the decor pool: they only generate near chamber entrances and at intersections respectively.

> Dispensers in this category are controlled by loot table dispensers/trial_chambers/chamber. (See § Chamber dispenser for loot table.)

> Other loot containers that belong to categories trial_chambers/chests and trial_chambers/reward.
