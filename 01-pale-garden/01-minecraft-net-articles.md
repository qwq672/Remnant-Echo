**原文引用：**

> If you’ve been counting down the minutes until you could test some of the unsettling features from our fourth game drop – I have good news for you! Today is that day for Java players, and it’s coming really soon for Bedrock players, too. Luckily, I’m not scared of anything, so I’ll be your guide as we take a deeper dive into the eerie new features revealed at Minecraft LIVE. Let’s go, brave explorers!

> Revealed at Minecraft Live, the pale garden is a new and mysterious biome coming to the Overworld. Animal mobs won't spawn here, but step inside and you'll discover groves of pale oak trees, curtains of hanging moss, and a startling new foe that calls this unsettling setting home: the creaking. Wait, did you hear that? Errr… I’m sure it was nothing!

> There are lots of pale oak trees in the pale garden, but there is also one sneaky mob that tries to disguise itself as a tree. The creaking is actually a startling new foe that’ll stand completely motionless when spotted by a player! Looking away is another story though – as this unsettling mob is surprisingly fast, and unsurprisingly dangerous…

> OK, maybe I lied a bit when I said I wasn’t scared. But at least I have my trusty sword with me! After all, what mob can’t be killed with a good ol’ enchanted sword?

> As it turns out, a creaking can’t be defeated by attacking it directly. Don’t ask me how I discovered this, or if I’m OK. I’m definitely not.

> The only way to defeat a creaking is to find its creaking heart – and believe me, they’re not that easy to find when you’re running for your hearts through a dense pale garden. While creaking hearts might look like wood, these are actually living blocks – and they hide a secret that you'll want to uncover much more quickly than I did.

> But it’s not all panicked running, screaming, and button-mashing in the pale garden. Visit during the daytime, and there’s nothing to stop you peacefully bring a slice of the it back to your base – with the pale oak wood set! Craft boats, fences, trapdoors, and more from this silvery-white new wood block. You might want to leave that creaking heart behind though...

> Phew, okay – our tour is almost over and I still have a few hearts left – so let’s talk pale moss! Found in the pale garden, pale moss is a white moss variant that can cover nearby blocks and be spread using bone meal. I think I’ll be gathering some up to use as carpet for my new greenhouse. Yippee!



### Beyond the pale garden
- **来源类型**：minecraft.net
- **原文链接**：<https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/beyond-the-pale-garden>

- **页面摘要**：Venture beyond the pale garden with community challenges, a live event, and more! Nab three eerie add-ons today, including Phantom Frames (free on Bedrock and also available as a Java mod). What surprises await tomorrow? Check back in to find out!

**原文引用：**

> Before we continue this live reporting from the pale garden, we want to address yesterday's broadcast in which our reporter Sophie disappeared after encountering a creaking heart. Many of you have expressed concern and while Sophie hasn't yet been found, neither has her inventory, which means our search continues. Some might say no news is good news, but we're a news channel, so we'll keep reporting. That's what Sophie would've wanted.

> While we continue to investigate her disappearance, we will also cover two heart-racing community challenges, a live event that hits very close to home for us at Mojang Studios, plus a look at the latest fashion straight from the runway of the pale garden. Or wait, does it say running away from the pale garden? We’ll have to get back to you on that.

> For today’s top story, we head to Minecraft Marketplace, where the new eerie add-ons have shaken the community. Let’s take a look.

> Many walls have had a makeover since the free Phantom Frames add-on was released this morning. Art is known to transport you to other places, but the players of this add-on claim to have seen somebody physically walk through a painting – only to never return! Which one of the many variations of frames was involved has not yet been confirmed. Investigators are instead busy following up reported sightings of the player in other biomes but are so far unsuccessful. Instead, a rumor is spreading amongst locals where some claim to have seen somebody move inside said paintings.

> So are these frames cursed or are they haunted? We tried to track down the merchant selling these paintings to get an interview, but apparently, they only come out at night which, due to what's now called “the Sophie incident”, we don’t.

> Moving over to cozier news. Today, several players have been seen venturing far into the pale garden for some... well, unexpected camping. To many, a somewhat eerie biome would not have been the first choice to spend the night, but these happy campers beg to differ. They have come equipped with not only hammocks and tents in various colors, but flashlights and a mob detector. “Nothing beats telling ghost stories around a campfire in the dead of night,” says one of these brave players. And we have to admit, eating s’mores under the northern lights does sound appealing. Let’s just hope the creaking doesn’t think so too.

> Speaking of ghost stories, players were in for a fluffy but ghastly surprise today as adoptions for ghost pets opened. Unsure whether to cuddle or cry, consensus among pet parents-to-be seems to be that these glowing ghouls are quite the charmers. Experts, however, are concerned that this will likely skyrocket the already substantial cases of pet hoarding within Minecraft communities and do urge those in the risk group to act with caution. Since ghost pets have been reported to come with unexpected abilities and do require you to summon them with a wand, we deem this advice wise, albeit unlikely to be followed by the masses.

> That’s all for tonight. We’ll see you right back here tomorrow when Sophie will hopefully have been found, but otherwise Cristina will be the one to bring us the newest news from not one but two community challenges. I’m Linn Viberg, and thanks for reading.



### Minecraft Snapshot 24w40a
- **来源类型**：minecraft.net
- **原文链接**：<https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/minecraft-snapshot-24w40a>
- **发布时间**：2024-10-01T22:00:00.000+0000
- **页面摘要**：Minecraft Snapshot 24w40a

**原文引用：**

> In this week's snapshot we are adding a new experiment which will allow you to experience the Pale Garden, a new eerie biome filled with Pale Oak Trees and Hanging Moss. Beware of its sole inhabitant, the Creaking, and don't blink!

> The experimental feature with the Pale Garden biome can be enabled by turning the "Winter Drop" experiment on. You can do this in the Experiments screen while creating the world. These experiments have no effect unless enabled. You can find more information about Feature Toggles here.

> We would love to hear your feedback on the new Pale Garden biome and the hostile mob the Creaking.

> Pale Hanging Moss generates naturally in the Pale Garden biome under the foliage of Pale Oaks

> The Creaking mob spawns at night in the Pale Garden biome if there is a Creaking Heart block that has been generated in a Pale Oak tree. Gameplay features:

> A Creaking Heart is a “living” block generating in Pale Oak trees, covered on all sides by Pale Oak wood logs. It can spawn a Creaking mob when it is nighttime, and it is placed between two correctly aligned Pale Oak log blocks.

> Ambient sounds in the Pale Garden biome do not come from the biome settings but are generated by blocks found in the biome
