### Happy ghast
- **来源类型**：minecraft.net

- **页面摘要**：What’s that, flying over there on the horizon? Is it a bird? Is it a plane? No, it's the happy ghast - here to teach you all there is to know about this wholesome mob!

**原文引用：**

> What’s that, flying over there on the horizon? Is it a bird? Is it a plane? No, you fool, because neither of those things exist in Minecraft. Well, I suppose chickens are birds, but they don’t really fly. Unless you drop them off a cliff. And I suppose parrots are birds too, and they kinda fly, though not very far.

> Oh, and then there are phantoms, which are probably not birds but definitely do fly. And then there are allays. And bees. And bats. And the Ender dragon. Actually quite a lot of things fly in Minecraft. But we’re getting distracted here, and the thing that was on the horizon is now almost on top of us. Fortunately, it’s our mob of the month – the happy ghast – and you can bet that it’s pleased to see you!

> Happy ghasts are not your average ghast. Announced at Minecraft Live 2025 and released in the Chase the Skies update in June 2025, they’re not only happier than their terrifying cousins, but can also be ridden by up to four players at once, and even tow cargo along with them.

> I’m sure it hasn’t escaped your notice that most ghasts are not particularly happy, so how can you cheer one up? Well, the answer is a bit involved. First, you’ll need to go to the Nether, and dig around or barter with the locals until you find a dried ghast – which is either the shrivelled up remains of an old ghast or some kind of ghast egg or something else entirely. Mojang’s top biologists are still trying to get to the bottom of that. One of our science team did find out, though, that dried ghasts can be crafted by mashing together eight ghast tears and a block of soul sand in a crafting bench. We weren’t sure whether to fire them or give them a raise after that particular discovery.

> However you acquire it, bring your dried ghast back to the Overworld and plop it down in some shallow water. Green particles will appear, and the rehydration process will begin. You’ll see the lil creature get brighter and happier over time. After about 20 minutes, it’ll perk up enough to begin flying – at this stage, it’s known as a ghastling (not to be confused with baby ghasts, which are a different stage of the ghast lifecycle). Look after your ghastling, and feed it snowballs to accelerate its growth, and eventually it’ll mature into an adult happy ghast.

> Once grown up, a happy ghast will fly around the area where it grew up. If you approach one holding a harness or a snowball, it’ll fly down to you – at which point you can equip the ghast with a harness and up to four players can ride it. The first player that mounts the ghast gets to steer, while the others can sit on its back and enjoy the views. It can fly both horizontally and vertically – point in the direction you want to go and move forward.

> Happy ghasts can also carry extra cargo with them, using leads. Boats, boats with chests, horses, camels, sniffers, and a few other things can be easily attached to a harnessed happy ghast by sneaking while holding a leash and hitting the use key on the ghast. To remove the leash again, use a pair of shears. Sadly, you can’t ride in a boat carried by a ghast (we wouldn’t want you to fall out at a great height), but you can ride a pig or unsaddled horse, so that’s fun.

> When you’re done flying around, hit the dismount button and you’ll be standing on top of the ghast. The ghast will hang out like this for as long as you stand on it – making it easy to build tall structures. But be aware that if you jump off then it might wander away, so be sure to tether it to a post or something.

### Ghast

- **发布时间**：2024-01-04T16:29:30.171+0000
- **页面摘要**：Creeping through the Nether in search of its goodies is a terrifying task at the best of times. Avoiding pigmen, seas of lava, blazes and wither skeletons is a full-time job, but nothing beats a ghast. In this article, we look closer at this fire-spitting mob!

**原文引用：**

> Creeping through the Nether in search of its goodies is a terrifying task at the best of times. Avoiding pigmen, seas of lava, blazes and wither skeletons is a full-time job. But then you hear a sound. A little whine, at a distance. Oh no. It’s a ghast – and it’s our mob of the month.

> Ghasts are large, incorporeal beings that float around the Nether. It’s not clear where they came from, or why, but they’ve been sighted on the unpleasant side of obsidian portals ever since the Nether dimension was added to the game in October 2010, as part of the Halloween Update.

> You’ll only encounter ghasts in the basalt deltas, Nether wastes and soul sand valley biomes – they tend to avoid the fungal forests where it’s harder to track their prey. Most of the time they just bob around, with their legs, or tentacles, or whatever they are, dangling below them and their eyes tightly closed.

> If they sense a player, though, their eyes open wide and they’ll begin to spit fireballs. These projectiles can destroy blocks and set whatever they hit on fire. Except a blaze, which is already ablaze. Ghasts keep their distance, making it hard to take them down with a sword – you’ll usually want a bow or crossbow instead.

> Ghasts are affected by poison, but having spent so long in the Nether they’ve become immune to heat damage. In fact, they love the warmth of lava – and if they pass under a lava fall or sink into a pool of the stuff, they’ll slow down a little to enjoy it. This is a great time to pepper them with arrows.

> If you do manage to take down one of these beasts, it’ll drop three things – gunpowder, ghast tears, and experience orbs, in varying quantities. The gunpowder can be used to craft TNT, fireworks or fire charges. The experience is used for enchanting. And the tears can be combined with an Awkward Potion to make a Potion of Regeneration – extremely useful when you’re traversing the Nether.

> My top tip for killing a ghast? Swinging your sword at exactly the right time when a fireball is approaching will bat it right back where it came from, hopefully killing the ghast and earning you a “Return to Sender” achievement to boot! It’s not easy, but nothing ever is – especially not in the Nether.

### Ghast Tear
- **来源类型**：minecraft.net
- **原文链接**：<https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/ghast-tear>
- **发布时间**：2024-07-24T22:00:00.000+0000
- **页面摘要**：Ghast tears were added in version 1.0.0 of Minecraft – the Adventure Update, which was also the first version to include brewing, enchanting, breeding, and many features that today are very much core to the game. In this article, we look closer at it.

**原文引用：**

> Have you ever wondered why ghasts are so weepy? I have. Back in 2017, the inimitable Tom Stone published some theories, including that “No one wants to 'hang' with a fire-breathing ghost”, and “It's never once managed to eat an ice cream without melting it before it even got close to its mouth.”

> I don’t know what the real reason is, but my theory is that it’s because we’ve never covered ghast tears in our Taking Inventory column. So let’s fix that, crown them our item of the month, and ensure that no ghast ever cries again. Wait... hold on... that doesn’t sound like a good idea...

> Ghast tears were added in version 1.0.0 of Minecraft – the Adventure Update, which was also the first version to include brewing, enchanting, breeding, and many features that today are very much core to the game.

> It should not be a surprise to learn that you can get ghast tears from ghasts, but I have good and bad news related to that. The good news is that you don’t need to climb up to one of the huge, creepy, flying, fireball-spitting beasts and scrape a tear off their face. The bad news is that you’ll have to defeat one (or more) of them in combat instead.

> Every time you slay a ghast, there’s a chance that a ghast tear will drop – and that chance increases with every level of Looting enchantment on your weapon. At maximum, you’ve got the chance of collecting four ghast tears at once from a particularly sad ghast. The Looting enchantment also works when you defeat a ghast by deflecting one of its fireballs with an enchanted weapon.

> But be careful because the tear will drop below where the ghast perished. Quite often in the Nether, that means over a big lake of lava – and if the tear falls in there, you’re unlikely to be getting it out without roasting yourself to a crisp in the process. Try to defeat a ghast when it’s flying over land instead.

> Why would you even go to all the trouble of dueling with a ghast anyway? Well, it turns out that ghast tears are a vital ingredient in one of the most useful potions in the game. Combine a ghast tear with an awkward potion in a brewing stand, and you’ll get yourself a potion of regeneration, which will slowly but surely restore your hearts over time. It’s a great way of keeping your health topped up during a long fight.

> The other thing you can do with a ghast tear is to combine it with glass and an eye of ender in a crafting table to create an End crystal. These dangerously unstable crystals are the only way to respawn the Ender Dragon after it has been defeated, if for some reason that’s a thing you want to do. Craft four of them, and place them on top of the flat sides of the exit portal to resurrect the dragon.
